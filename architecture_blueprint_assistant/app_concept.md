# Application Concept – SnapCircle

## Application
Collaborative event photo album for groups, members and guests.

## Core Features
- Shared event albums with invite links and QR codes
- Guest uploads without an account
- Quick filters and edits while uploading
- Real-time album feed with reactions and comments
- Creator settings: group size, album size, photos per person
- Easy member growth with in-app plan upgrades
- Freemium monetization: free tier with a set photo limit, paid tiers by group and album size

## Users
- Group Owners: create groups, set limits, manage billing
- Members: upload, edit and keep albums in their account
- Guests: join an event by invite link to upload and view
- Admins: oversee platform and moderate content

## Constraints
- Scale to 50K concurrent users with event upload spikes
- GDPR and PCI-DSS compliance (via payment provider)
- Mobile-first design (iOS, Android, web)
