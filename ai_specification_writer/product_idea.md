# Product Idea

## Vision
Help people live healthier lives by making it simple to track what they eat, understand their nutrition, and build lasting habits toward their weight and fitness goals.

## Users
- **Weight-loss seekers**: want an easy way to count calories and stay within a daily target to lose weight steadily.
- **Fitness enthusiasts and athletes**: want to track macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat) to support muscle gain, performance, and recovery.
- **Health-conscious individuals**: want visibility into their diet (sugar, sodium, fiber, vitamins) to eat better and manage conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure.
- **Busy professionals**: want fast, low-effort food logging that fits into a hectic schedule.
- **Dietitians and coaches**: want to monitor clients' food logs and progress to give informed guidance.

## Key Features
- Food diary with a large, searchable food database
- Barcode scanning for instant logging of packaged foods
- Personalized calorie and macronutrient goals based on age, weight, activity level, and objective
- Exercise logging with calories burned added to the daily budget
- Recipe importer and meal builder to calculate nutrition for home-cooked dishes
- Progress tracking with weight, body measurements, and charts over time
- Integration with wearables and fitness apps (e.g., Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin)
- Water intake tracking and daily reminders
- Community forums, friends, and challenges for motivation and accountability

## Differentiators
- One of the largest crowd-sourced and verified food databases, including restaurant and regional foods
- Quick-add tools (barcode scan, meal scan, recent and saved meals) that cut logging time
- Wide ecosystem of integrations that centralizes nutrition and activity data in one place
- Insights and nudges that turn raw logs into actionable advice (e.g., "You're low on protein today")
- Free core experience with a premium tier for advanced macro control, meal plans, and ad-free use
